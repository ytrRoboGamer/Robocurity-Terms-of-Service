This Privacy Policy explains how Robocurity ("the bot") handles user data. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about what information is collected and how it is used.

The bot may collect limited data to provide its features. This may include:

Data is stored securely and only for as long as necessary to operate the bot. We do not share or sell user data to any third parties.
